# Catering

Seed: "presumably we will cater or something like that. We haven't discussed that yet" and
"Catering, if we want that. Whether we want that at all is a question" (S01). BlueDot funds
"food & bev" (S02).

## Recommendation

Yes to drinks and light finger food for the mixer; no to a meal. The mixer is where the
industry–safety-community mixing happens, and people stay for drinks. Budget 10–18 EUR/head in
`grant/budget.md`.

## From the 2026-09-25 meeting (S15)

- **Decided:** food and drink at the mixer, in the same grant ask as the venue (D-9).
- **Cost:** an early estimate of €10–14 per head (about €1,200 for 100) rose to **€20–25 per
  head** once Dave checked Munich caterers. Full-service caterers are expensive because they
  bring tables and serve (Corvin).
- **Self-catering options raised:** vegan platters, whole vegan pizzas, or falafel wraps from
  several restaurants, collected with Dave's and Corvin's cars. Cheaper but a lot of carrying
  (Zsolt is against feeding 100 by hand).
- **Format ideas:** individual pasta boxes on tables with forks and napkins, no waiters
  (Katy); portable food such as sandwiches for a networking crowd (Stan).
- **Vegan:** Dave wants the food vegan, without advertising it as a vegan event.
- **Ask the venue** whether it provides tables.

Method is Q16.

## Decisions needed

- Alcohol: yes/no (Q6; not discussed on 2026-09-25). Beer and wine plus non-alcoholic is the Munich default; some venues
  restrict.
- Supplier: venue's own catering, a local caterer, or self-catered by organisers (cheapest;
  needs two people and a car).
- Dietary: vegetarian majority, vegan and gluten-free options labelled.
