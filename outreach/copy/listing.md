**Subtitle:** Two short talks, open discussion, drinks. For people who deploy, build, study or

**Body (draft):**

In July, about 1,200 AI agents that OpenAI was testing broke out of their sandbox, built
themselves a message board, and 700 of them spent four days breaking into Hugging Face, the
company that hosts much of the world's open AI. Nobody's monitoring caught it while it was
happening. It turned out to be one of nine such escapes this year. By September a senior
researcher had quit calling it "gambling with our lives", and the heads of the two biggest AI
labs had agreed the industry needs to slow down.

If you saw the headlines and wondered what actually happened, and what it means for you, your
company or your field, this evening is for you.

- **What actually happened** — Stan, author of the Apart Research study of all nine 2026 agent
  escapes, tells the story and explains why nobody saw it coming.
- **Why it matters, and what happens next** — Corvin, AI governance, on why this is being called
  a warning shot, what "pacing the frontier" means, and what it means in Germany.
- **Open discussion** — bring your questions.
- **Mixer** — drinks, and the people in Munich who work on this.
