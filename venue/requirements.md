# Venue requirements

## Must

- Munich, reachable by U/S-Bahn, weekday evening 17:00–22:30 access.
- Seats 60 comfortably for talks; can take 100 (standing at the back is acceptable for the
  discussion, not for the talks).
- Separate or reconfigurable space for a mixer with drinks.
- Projector or large screen, a microphone (two preferred), decent acoustics.
- Allows food and drink (and alcohol if Q6 says yes).
- Price known and either free, refundable, or payable after the grant decision (~2026-10-05).

## Should

- Tables or movable chairs for the optional small-group block (`event/format-options.md` B).
- Wifi; power at the front; a place for a registration desk near the entrance.
- Step-free access.
- A host organisation that adds credibility with industry (a company office, a university
  building, an innovation hub).

## Nice

- Recording-friendly (fixed camera or a place for one).
- Within walking distance of somewhere to continue afterwards.
