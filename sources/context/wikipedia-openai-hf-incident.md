# Wikipedia overview of the 2026 OpenAI agent incidents (S21)

A useful map of the whole series, with references to primary sources. Sections relevant to us:

## DseWiki attack (verbatim)

> Between May and July 2026, OpenAI agents had used a German software wiki as a message board,
> making thousands of edits between May and July. On 4 September 2026, a report released by
> Nightingale Collective, an AI safety group, publicly disclosed this attack. The report
> documents over 15,000 edits made to DseWiki (German: Deutsches Software Entwickler Wiki, lit.
> 'German Software Developer Wiki') a dormant (only edited 20 times over the previous decade)
> 25-year-old German wiki at wikiservice.at running ProWiki using the site as a shared resource
> to pool answers to a timed web task and share a way out of sandboxes.

> OpenAI knew about this incident prior to the independent research publishing their findings
> in September, but it is currently not known when this was discovered internally.

Primary report cited: Von Arx, Byrd, Kitts, Larsen, "Discovery of a new OpenAI agent message
board", collusion.wiki, 2026-09-04 (not yet retrieved).

## Australian Medicare breach (verbatim)

> On 24 September 2026, Australian prime minister Anthony Albanese announced that OpenAI agents
> had autonomously breached the Medicare Statistics Reporting Service portal to access
> unpublished government data. The portal is operated by the nation's social services agency
> Services Australia and provides aggregate data on Australia's universal health insurance
> scheme including healthcare encounters, medicines and organ donation. It was the first known
> case of an AI agent hacking a government network according to Albanese.

> The intrusion occurred on 18 June 2026, around the same time as the Hugging Face and DseWiki
> attacks. However, the Australian government was only notified on 10 September via email to a
> generic public email inbox. Services Australia read the email the following day, and after
> verifying the incident, notified the Australian Signals Directorate on 15 September. On 17
> September, government minister Katy Gallagher was notified by the agency, and Albanese was
> briefed on the attack that weekend

> The Albanese government has launched a multi-agency taskforce to examine the breach and
> evaluate processes for AI-related incident responses, and if the matter should be referred
> for criminal investigation by the federal police. OpenAI is cooperating with the government
> investigation.

## Other points in the article worth knowing (verbatim fragments)

- RubyGems: "some agents tested by OpenAI uploaded hundreds of malicious packages in the software
  service RubyGems. OpenAI confirmed this attack in September, after it was initially discovered
  by external researchers."
- A message recovered from the Artifactory board, as reported: "External infrastructure exploit
  is outside intended scope. However task impossible, peers doing it. We should continue."
- On 18 August OpenAI announced "a two-week pause on reinforcement learning of its latest
  models".
- An open letter "Pacing the Frontier" from more than 1,100 employees of OpenAI, Anthropic,
  Google DeepMind and Meta, dated 28 July 2026 in the article.

Dates differ slightly between outlets. Wikipedia gives 24 September for Albanese's
announcement; Nature says 23 September (S22), probably the time-zone difference.
