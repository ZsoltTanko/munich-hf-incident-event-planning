# AI Incident Response Sprint (S13)

The weekend hackathon where Stan's paper (S04) was produced. Theme: turning evidence from an
autonomous AI breach into defensive tools and regulatory responses. Online and in-person.

- Incidents studied: the July 2026 Hugging Face intrusion (07-11..13) and the May 2026 German
  wiki manipulation (~15,000 unauthorised edits).
- Tracks: Containment (verifiable sandbox-escape prevention); Reconstruction & Forecasting;
  Regulatory Response (EU AI Office documentation); Communication (incident disclosure
  effectiveness); Open.
- Resources given to participants: Hugging Face technical timeline (S10); OpenAI's account
  (S11); Discord discord.gg/XswWBvugYs; sprints@apartresearch.com.
- CeSIA: judging, reading materials, distribution via newsletter and a 7,000-member Discord,
  regulatory pathways. Prize pool $2,000 plus fellowship fast-tracks.

## Why it matters for our event

- Two primary sources for speakers (S10, S11) came from here.
- Apart and CeSIA are potential co-promoters or a source of a third speaker; the sprint's
  "Communication" track is literally about explaining these incidents to the public.
