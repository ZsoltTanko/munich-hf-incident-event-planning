# BlueDot Rapid Grants (S06)

"Up to $20,000 for time and resources to make progress" in AI safety or biosecurity.

## Categories

Research and learning; projects and experiments; **events and community (meetups, venues)**;
travel and collaboration.

## Eligibility

People pursuing "a promising next step in AI safety or biosecurity". Higher likelihood for
BlueDot course participants, alumni, facilitators or active community members.

## Restrictions (quoted)

"Rapid Grants cannot be used for party-political donations or campaigning, activities that are
unlawful in the UK or your jurisdiction, or activities outside BlueDot's charitable or
educational purposes." Cannot fund people or organisations based in Russia, China or India, or
anything that would breach sanctions.

## Process

1. Apply: plan and budget, about 15 minutes (the Airtable form, S07).
2. Review: BlueDot evaluates and emails a decision. (The RFE asks for a week; S02.)
3. Receive: submit a claim through the portal; **single lump-sum payment** after checks.
   Exchange and transfer fees may reduce the amount received.

## After award (quoted)

"Use the funding for the agreed purpose and get our written approval before making material
changes." "Send a short completion report within 60 days of finishing the work or the grant
period ending." Grantees get community access (introductions, event invites).

## Implications for us

- Budget in USD on the form; keep the EUR working budget and the rate assumption in
  `grant/budget.md`. Expect a few percent lost to conversion.
- Payment comes after award and claim, so the venue deposit may need to be fronted by an
  organiser or the venue must accept payment after the award email's timing (S02 says to
  arrange spending around it).
- A change of date, venue size or headcount after award is a "material change" needing written
  approval. Decide before applying, or state ranges in the application.
- The completion report template lives in `grant/completion-report-template.md`.
