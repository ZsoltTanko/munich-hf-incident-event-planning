# Rapid Small Grants blog post, November 2025 (S20)

An earlier description of Rapid Grants, when they were for BlueDot course participants and mostly
$50–$1,500. It describes a **reimbursement** model: submit a proposal, complete the project, then
"Claim reimbursement. We process most claims within 5 working days." Also: "At all times, only
assume we'll cover costs we've confirmed in writing for your specific project."

This probably explains the team's belief that the grant is a refund (Q18). The current FAQ (S06,
2026-09-25) describes "a single lump sum after you submit a claim", with timing set in the
acceptance email. The current page takes precedence; the blog post is older.
