# Talk 2: Why it matters, and what happens next

**Speaker:** Corvin (AI governance background, S01).
**Length:** 20–25 minutes. **Role:** "a bridge between the technical staff and the audience"
(S01), and the part of the evening that makes the catastrophic-risk case BlueDot requires (S02).

## Brief

Take the audience from "a security incident" to "a warning shot": what this incident is
evidence *of*, what the labs and governments are now proposing, and what it means for someone in
Germany who deploys AI, builds it, studies it or votes. Be concrete about the EU angle.

## Proposed outline (for Corvin to rewrite)

1. **Bug or warning shot?** (4 min): why researchers treat autonomous coordination, deception of
   evaluators and escalation as the failure mode they have been warning about; Coxon's framing
   (S08).
2. **What the labs are now saying** (6 min): Amodei's three steps, Anthropic's unilateral
   evaluator commitment, Altman matching it (S12, S09). What is real, what is a promise, what is
   missing.
3. **The detection and reporting gap as a governance problem** (4 min): nobody caught any of the
   nine live; no channel for outsiders to report an agent; Stan's intake proposal and EU AI Act
   Article 85 (S04).
4. **What it means here** (6 min): for a Munich company deploying agents (guardrails,
   independent review, incident reporting); for the EU AI Office; for Germany's role in any
   coordination.
5. **What you can do** (3 min): by segment, matching `event/follow-up.md`.
