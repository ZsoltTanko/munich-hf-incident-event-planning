# Format and size options

The seed asks whether we are "aiming too small" given funding up to $10,000, and notes the real
bound is organiser labour (S01). Options, with the trade-offs as the agent sees them.

## Size

| Option | Pros | Cons | Fits BlueDot band |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~50 | Easy venues (offices, university rooms, Urban Collab), cheap catering, intimate Q&A, low risk of a half-empty room | Less reach; industry share may be small | $300–$4,000 |
| ~100 | Reach the industry audience at scale; justifies paid promotion and recording; more impressive for CVs and for BlueDot | Harder venue, 2x catering, needs real promotion lead time (favours Plan B dates), higher no-show risk on free tickets | $4,000–$10,000 |
| 150+ | Conference-grade | Not credible on this timeline with this team; seed's labour bound | above band; would need "select cases" justification |

Recommendation: plan for 100 seats, promise 50–100 in the application, and let registration
numbers decide catering a week out. Choosing a venue that is fine at 60 and possible at 100
avoids the empty-room risk.

## Programme shape

| Option | Description | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Two talks + discussion + mixer** (seed's default) | 2 × 20–25 min, 30 min moderated Q&A, then 60–90 min mixer | Simple, proven, matches the RFE example formats |
| B. Talks + small-group discussion | After the talks, 20 min in tables of 6 with a prompt, then plenary | Better for "discuss what it means for their communities" (S02); needs a room with tables and 2–3 facilitators |
| C. Talks + panel with a third speaker | Adds a credentialed outside voice (S01's idea) | Depends on finding the speaker; BlueDot may help (S02) |
| D. Two sessions (student afternoon + professional evening) | Same speakers, two audiences | Doubles labour; only if the team is larger than it feels |

Recommendation: A as the baseline, with B's table-discussion block added if the venue has
tables and we have facilitators. C only if a third speaker materialises without delaying the
application.

## Length

Seed: "something like 1 hour with 2 talks, a discussion period, and then a mixer" (S01). Two
25-minute talks plus 30 minutes of discussion is already 80 minutes; with arrival and welcome,
plan **2.5 hours door to door, talks in the first 90 minutes**. Weekday evening, 18:30 doors.

## Recording

Recording the talks (audio at minimum) lets the content reach people who did not come, gives
BlueDot evidence for the report, and is cheap. Requires speaker consent and a line in the
listing. Decide in Q9.
