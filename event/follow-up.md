# Follow-up

BlueDot asks for "any next steps or follow-up you plan" (S02) and a completion report within 60
days (S06). We should commit to a few things we will actually do, not a long list of maybes.

## What every attendee gets

1. **A one-page resource sheet** (printed at the event, emailed after): the three-sentence
   summary (D01), the timeline, links to the METR investigation (S03), the Hugging Face timeline
   (S10), Stan's paper (S04), Amodei's essay (S12), and local groups.
2. **The slides and, if recorded, the talks.**
3. **A short survey**: what you understood before/after; what you will do differently; would you
   come to a follow-up. Feeds the completion report.

## Next steps by segment (offer, do not push)

| Segment | Offer |
|---|---|
| Industry / engineers | A short checklist: questions to ask before deploying agents (tool scope, credential isolation, monitoring, kill switches, independent review), drawn from Hugging Face's hardening list (S10). Optional: a follow-up session or a private round-table for companies. |
| Founders | Introductions to local safety-minded people at the mixer; pointer to BlueDot's community. |
| Students | BlueDot courses (S02, S06); Apart Research sprints (S13); a local reading group if one exists or if EA Munich will host one. |
| Everyone | EA Munich, ACX Munich, PauseAI Munich meetups; BlueDot's Luma events. |

## What we commit to in the application (proposal; pick in Q10)

- Resource email within 3 days.
- Survey and attendance numbers to BlueDot in the completion report.
- One of: (a) a follow-up discussion evening in ~4–6 weeks, (b) a reading group seeded with
  attendees, hosted by a local group, or (c) an industry round-table if ≥10 industry attendees
  ask for one.
